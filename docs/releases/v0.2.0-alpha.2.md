# PackTrace v0.2.0-alpha.2

I am publishing the next **experimental iteration of the 0.2.0 cycle**, grouping
pnpm version/layout coverage since [v0.2.0-alpha.1](v0.2.0-alpha.1.md). This is not
production qualification or complete pnpm support. Application versioning follows
the [release-level policy](README.md); report schema **1.7** is unchanged.

## Accumulated pnpm improvements

- I recognize canonical stable **pnpm 9.x, 10.x, 11.x and 12.x** hints for the
  existing exact lockfile **9.0** interpretation. Exact tested producer versions
  are sample anchors, not an exclusive patch whitelist or a promise of every
  patch/layout. CLI/policy profiles remain `pnpm` / `pnpm@lockfile-9`, not concrete
  producer versions; prerelease, malformed and future13 hints remain unqualified.
- I retain owned Linux/amd64 producer anchors **9.0.0, 9.15.9, 10.0.0, 10.34.9,
  11.28.9 and native12.18.0**, covering isolated/hoisted copy, distinct-peer and
  actual hardlink-refusal cases. The 24 raw captures are exercised by **84
  profile/hint replays**, including separately authored12 hints without changing
  the original producer manifests.
- I adapt the approved native12 fixture producer without installer/downloads,
  obsolete direct flags or unintended self-resolution. Historical failed12
  attempts remain recorded separately; successful follow-up evidence does not
  rewrite the earlier failures or renew either closed campaign.
- I strengthen replay checks for exact readable/unreadable/candidate counts and
  distinct parent locations under peer contexts. These are not physical lock
  joins, authenticated origins or unique peer bindings.
- I synchronize English/Spanish manuals and present compatibility ranges
  separately from exact producer anchors. See the [version/layout matrix](../inventory-compatibility.md#pnpm-versionlayout-matrix).

## Boundaries remain unchanged

Installed observations require active contained stable single-link manifests;
that means nlink1, not one symlink hop. Generic hardlinks remain unreadable,
including the five unreadable observations in each forced-hardlink sample.
The exact active PNPM store shape and native/selected-lock witnesses are retained.
All three mandatory installed/reconciliation/workspace gaps, one incomplete scope
and expected exit3 remain. Correspondence is **candidate-only and indeterminate**.
There is no new pnpm workspace/shared-store access, advisory lookup, integrity
binding, exception or source-authentication authority. Existing npm/Bun behavior,
saved policies, setup and intelligence state are not migrated or repinned.

## Verify before use

Obtain the intended archive, `build-metadata.json` and `SHA256SUMS` from this same
prerelease. In the download directory, Linux/Nushell-compatible commands are:

```nu
sha256sum --ignore-missing --check SHA256SUMS
tar -xzf packtrace_0.2.0-alpha.2_linux_amd64.tar.gz
./packtrace_0.2.0-alpha.2_linux_amd64/packtrace --version
```

Require both the intended archive and build manifest to report `OK`; missing files
are not verified by `--ignore-missing`. On Windows, compare exact ZIP/manifest
entries with PowerShell `Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256`, then extract normally.
Do not execute an unverified archive merely to inspect its version. Expected output:
`packtrace 0.2.0-alpha.2 (experimental; not production-qualified)`.

The archives install no service, fetch no intelligence and execute no target
scripts. Installing this executable does not authorize new targets/source access,
repin policies or refresh saved snapshots. Zero findings never certifies safety.
The [English manual](../user-guide.md) / [manual en español](../user-guide.es.md)
retain exit precedence and offline setup/policy guidance.
